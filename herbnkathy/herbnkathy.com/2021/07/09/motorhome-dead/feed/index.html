Candy, so sorry to hear about the pink eye and hope all is well by now. Regarding the water in the fresh water tank. If you have a 3-way valve (supply/tank/feed to rig) then I would suspect that maybe that valve wasn’t set in the “City Water” position fully and allowed most of the water to go to the rig plumbing but some of the park supplied water to trickle into the tank. Not to worry, most if not all rigs have a fresh water tank overflow port so I suspect you would’ve seen some water on the site pavement as you pulled out? If your rig is like ours and you don’t have a 3-way valve then I’d suspect the park supply water is coming in to the rig plumbing system and some of that water is trickling backwards through the water pump check valve into the tank (and ultimately overflowing onto the pavement). I wouldn’t be too worried about it. If the next site you get to you DO NOT hook up to city water AND you have water under the rig — that’s not good then you have a leak.
Best of luck to you, great to meet you at Dale Hollow and we look forward to spending a little more time with you somewhere down the road.
