I think you’d appreciate the power, the airbag suspension, and quiet ride (the engines in the rear) of any diesel pusher (DP) over any gas motorhome .. that’s why we switched from a Monaco on Workhorse to Airstream on an Fliner with diesel. If we were to buy another MH it would be a DP for sure. Also one of the 99 to 2004 vintage. 99 was the first year for slides in RVs and we like our living room slide, although we could see ourselves living in one without, just not quite as comfortable. Are you going to live in it full time? If so, then look at storage space, especially closet space and pantry space. I’d be glad to talk with you anytime.
