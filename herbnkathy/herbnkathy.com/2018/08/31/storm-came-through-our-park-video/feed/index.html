Hadn’t seen it here before. Town is desimated, church roof torn, straight line winds broke trees halfway up. Lots of lines down, houses crushed, still no power lots of places, about 30 power contractor trucks all over town. We have our 8kw diesel generator built into the coach, so we just pushed the button and had power for coffee and tv everything else is 12 volt or propane so no worry. Fortunately we had water in our fresh water tank so we could just turn on the pump, since we are on well water and no electric means no water (as you are well aware) people were using pool water to flush their commodes
