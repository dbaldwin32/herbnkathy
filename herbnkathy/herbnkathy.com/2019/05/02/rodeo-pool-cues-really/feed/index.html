Their regular renewal rate was quoted at $149.95 for our recently expired Platinum (any RV) plan. So we went to the store and bought a new Standard (towables only) plan without using our Good Sam club membership for $64.95. We received a Roadside assistance card and the plan was registered in the store using a different phone # than what is on the GS club account. A few days later I called to get a free upgrade to the Platinum plan for our motorhome. This is an unofficial work around and may require the store manager to advocate on your behalf. No guarantees that it will work every time but worth a try.
